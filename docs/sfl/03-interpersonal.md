# Interpersonal: Who is involved?

## Interpersonal Metafunction (Tenor)

This focuses on the *social relationships* - the roles of the speakers/writers,
their attitudes, and how they interact. Crucially, it includes **Modality**.

**In `promptly.yml`:** `sfl_interpersonal:`

* **`intended_audience:`**: Who is the prompt/output for?
* **`tone:`**: The desired emotional or stylistic quality.
* **`modality_rules:`**: How *certain* should the language be?
    * **`process:`**: Links to an Ideational process.
    * **`certainty:`**: (e.g., high, medium, low, conditional).
    * **`rationale:`**: Why this level?
