# Introduction to SFL in Promptly

## Why Systemic Functional Linguistics (SFL)?

SFL helps us understand how language *works* in context. When crafting AI prompts,
thinking about SFL helps us be more precise, control the output better, and
reduce unintended biases.

`promptly` incorporates SFL through a structured YAML front matter.

**Key Idea:** Build prompts by defining *what* you're talking about,
*how* you're relating to the audience, and *how* the text should flow.

*Press 'n' for next, 'p' for previous, 't' for toc, 'q' to quit.*
